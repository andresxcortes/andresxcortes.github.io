## Context
Bitso, Latin America's largest crypto exchange, where I served in-house as Argentina CISO and Information Security PMO Manager. The Argentine operation came under a newly regulated national regime, where a virtual-asset licensing/registration framework (CNV RG 1058/2025) and formal security expectations came into force on fixed regulator deadlines.

## Problem
The organization had to translate a fresh regulatory framework into operational controls and produce regulator-ready evidence — while simultaneously pursuing an ISO 27001 certification, and without stalling a fast product-release cadence. Missing the deadlines carried registration and reputational risk.

## Approach
Ran the security and compliance workstream as a program: mapped the regulatory requirements to a concrete control set, built the evidence plan, and coordinated cross-functional delivery through a security PMO (Jira workflows, executive cadences, capacity planning). Sequenced the ISO 27001 gap-to-certification effort in parallel so the two programs reinforced rather than blocked each other.

## Outcome
Formal evidence packages were delivered within the regulator's deadlines and the ISO 27001 certification was achieved — with the operating mechanisms lifting cross-functional throughput on security work materially, and without slowing product release velocity.
