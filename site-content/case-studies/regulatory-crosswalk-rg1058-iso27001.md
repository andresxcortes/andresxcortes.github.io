Argentina's Virtual Asset Service Provider framework (**CNV RG 1058/2025**) mapped article-by-article to **ISO/IEC 27001:2022** controls — turning the regulatory text into an operational control set: for each article, a control objective, the associated controls, the ISO/IEC 27001:2022 reference, and the technical services it touches.

Most Argentine regulatory matrices focus on the **BCRA** (banking and payments). This one covers the **CNV crypto / VASP perimeter** and adds the layer that makes it actionable — the mapping to an international control standard a VASP is likely already implementing.
