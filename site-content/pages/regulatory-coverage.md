# LatAm Regulatory Coverage — Fintech & Crypto

The specific frameworks I work within across the region's three defining markets. Regulatory landscape as of 2026; frameworks in Argentina, Mexico, and Brazil continue to evolve.

<!-- Claude Code: render this as the branded matrix table (teal header, tinted country column).

| Market | Fintech & Payments | Crypto / Virtual Assets | AML / CFT (FIU) | Data Protection |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Argentina** | BCRA — Payment Service Providers (PSP / PSPCP) and payment accounts (Comunicaciones "A"). | CNV — PSAV registry; RG 1058/2025 (registration, minimum net worth, cybersecurity, custody, annual audit); Ley 27.739. | UIF — Ley 25.246 (amended by Ley 27.739); VASPs as obligated subjects. | Ley 25.326 (Personal Data Protection) — AAIP. |
| **Mexico** | Ley Fintech / LRITF (2018) — ITFs: IFPE (e-money) & IFC (crowdfunding); CNBV + Banxico; SPEI / CoDi. | Virtual assets only with prior Banxico authorization; restrictive perimeter for ITFs and banks. | LFPIORPI ("Ley Antilavado") — UIF under SHCP. | New LFPDPPP (2025) — Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno (replaced INAI). |
| **Brazil** | Lei 12.865/2013 — payment institutions; Pix and Open Finance (BCB). | Lei 14.478/2022 (BVAL); BCB Resolutions 519/520/521 (Nov 2025) — authorization, prudential rules, Travel Rule; BCB supervision. | Lei 9.613/1998 — COAF; VASPs as obligated entities. | LGPD (Lei 13.709/2018) — ANPD. |
